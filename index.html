SELECTED CASE STUDIES

How I approach delivery challenges

Each case highlights responsibility, decisions and approach—not just project description.

CASE 01Government ERP

Turning multi-department requirements into one ERP delivery plan

Challenge

Multiple processes and feedback streams across HR, finance, budgeting and warehouses.

Responsibility

Align priorities, control CRs, coordinate workstreams and drive testing/readiness.

Key Decision

Handle feedback through change control rather than disconnected individual fixes.

Outcome

Clearer delivery path, stronger testability/acceptance and less cross-module conflict.

CASE 02Investment Integration

Separating business transactions from accounting events

Challenge

The investment portal manages allocation/contracts/installments while ERP manages accounting and collection.

Responsibility

Define owner, business event, data object and the point where financial impact is created.

Key Decision

Prevent duplicate ownership by defining the system of record for each step.

Outcome

Clearer integration, stronger data ownership and reduced duplication/conflict.

CASE 03AI Delivery

Moving AI from chatbot to governed operational agent

Challenge

The agent can answer questions but cannot reliably execute enterprise procedures.

Responsibility

Design operating and test procedures covering scope, permissions, validation and file understanding.

Key Decision

Test the agent on outcomes rather than memorized step-following alone.

Outcome

A clearer framework for building agents that can be trained, tested and continuously improved.
